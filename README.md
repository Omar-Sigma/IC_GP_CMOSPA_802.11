# My undergraduate senior thesis

Fully-integrated 802.11 Power Amplifiers in 65nm Technology
- Designed two PAs for the 2.4GHz & 5GHz bands in 65nm. Both PAs integrate all passive elements.
- Output P1dB: 18.9dBm (2.4 → 2.5GHz) and 20.8dBm (5 → 5.8GHz).
- Gains of ≤ 26.7dB (2.4GHz) & ≤ 19.9dB (5GHz). Unconditional stability from 1Hz → 100GHz.
- Peak Power Added Efficiency: P AE ≈ 20.8%, 24.3% for the 2.4GHz & 5GHz PAs respectively.
- For a 64-QAM, 802.11n signal: EV M ≤ −22dB at Pout ≈ 15.4dBm, 15.3dBm for 2.4GHz & 5GHz.
- For a 256-QAM, 802.11ac signal: EV M ≤ −37dBEV M at Pout ≈ 11.3dBm (5GHz).
- The PAs demonstrated compliance with the spectral mask and OIIP3 of ≈ 39dBm, 40dBm respectively.
- Post-layout simulations (with PDK-provided passives) showed a degradation of −8dB in gain and
minimal degradation in OP1dB by about 1dB, with a PAE drop of 2 to 4%. With room for further tuning.
